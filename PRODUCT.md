# PRODUCT.md — Contour

register: product

## What it is
Contour is an aesthetic-clinic operating system (patients, consent, 3D facial
canvas, AI before/after visualization, bookings calendar, VYBERO voice-agent
integration, admin analytics) built by TechGIS for a network of aesthetic
clinics in Pakistan and the Philippines. First showcase clinic: Meridian
Aesthetics, Lahore; first network doctor: Dr Omer Irfan (Karachi).

## Users
- **Doctors** (consultant plastic surgeons / aestheticians): run consultations,
  review and approve everything patient-facing. Time-poor, brand-proud.
- **Front desk / nurses**: register patients, capture photos, manage the
  calendar, hand out the free Assessment Report.
- **Clinic admin**: staff, settings, analytics.
- **Patients** (indirectly): receive printed/PDF artefacts — the consultation
  report and the free Aesthetic Assessment Report. Urdu/English/Punjabi
  speakers; WhatsApp is the delivery channel.

## Tone & brand
Premium, calm, clinical-but-warm. "Precision aesthetics, beautifully
planned." Strengths-first language, never manufactured insecurity (hard
guardrail). The Contour mark: half female face, half wireframe mesh, mint.

## The Assessment Report (patient-facing artefact)
A free lead-magnet one-pager: a real clinical document the clinic can
WhatsApp to a lead minutes after a selfie. It must read as a premium
medical-aesthetic letterhead document (editorial, credible, kind), not a
SaaS dashboard export. The doctor reviews and can edit wording before it
leaves the building. Owner verdict on v1: "embarrassing" — v2 must be
something a clinic proudly sends.

## Anti-references
- SaaS dashboard aesthetics on patient-facing paper (stat cards, pill
  badges, progress bars everywhere).
- "AI report" look: generic gradients, robotic tone, walls of chips.
- Anything that reads as a scorecard of flaws; the document guides, it
  never grades the person.

## Strategic principles
- Booth-grade reliability: everything must work offline/keyless with
  graceful degradation.
- Deterministic over generative wherever trust matters (geometry math is
  ours; AI only classifies within locked taxonomies).
- One engine, per-clinic skins (logo, palette, name, booking link).
