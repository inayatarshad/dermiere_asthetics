# CAPTURE Clinic OS · 5-Minute Demo Script

The story: **one client journey, hello to glow** — booking, skin analysis,
visualization, payment, review, reward — across all four Lahore locations,
on one screen. Everything below is live software, no slides.

Have two windows ready: the app, and your phone (for the review link moment).

---

## 0. Open cold on the landing page (30s)

*The page is already CAPTURE: logo, noir & champagne, "The intimate science
of beauty."*

Say: "This is your operating system. Everything a client touches carries
your brand — starting with the receptionist who never sleeps."

## 1. VYBERO books a client (75s) — §5.1, the Saturday promise

Tap **VYBERO · concierge** (bottom right, no login needed).

Type these, in order (answers are instant, from your real knowledge base):
1. `Is the red light therapy painful? Any needles?` → needle-free story, SPICUS spicules
2. `How much is body contouring?` → Rs. 22,000/session, course of 6 at Rs. 118,000
3. `Book me a body contour session` → it walks the booking: location →
   day → **it offers only genuinely free slots from the live diary** →
   name → phone → confirm.

Say: "That booking just landed in the clinic calendar, and the conversation
is already in your analytics. WhatsApp is the same brain on your number."

## 2. The clinic side (60s)

Sign in as **Front Desk** (frontdesk@capture.cc / capture).

- **Dashboard**: today's queue — Mahnoor's glow session, Zara's consult.
- **Calendar**: point at the location chips — *"each of your four sites
  keeps its own diary — Experience Centre, Dr. Haroon, Dr. Nazia,
  Dr. Ashba"* — and the VYBERO-badged booking that just arrived.

## 3. MARK-VU + the Studio (75s) — §5.2 + §5.6

Open **Patients → Mahnoor Baig**.

- MARK-VU panel: *"your intake scanner's numbers, tracked across visits —
  pigmentation down 14, moisture up 17 since session one."*
- Tap **Visualization Studio** → the before/after stage with her photo.
  Drag the **Radiance** slider — the glow preview renders live, on-device.
  Switch to **Body Contour** → Abdomen preset, pull **Contour strength**.
- Point at the caption: *"every output is watermarked 'illustrative
  preview, not a guarantee' — brand-safe by construction."*
- (With an AI key configured, tap **Enhance with AI** for the photoreal pass.)

## 4. POS: the visit closes on paper (60s) — §5.3

Go to **Point of Sale**.

- Tap **Exomere Body Contour** into the cart, type client `Amina Shahid`
  (links to her record).
- *"Amina reviewed us last week — she earned a Capture Circle code."*
  Enter her code → **−10% applies, tax recalculates**.
- Charge → cash → **Record payment** → **Print A4 invoice** → the branded
  letterhead invoice opens print-ready: line items, Capture Circle
  discount line, 16% tax, the location's own invoice number.

## 5. The loop closes (60s) — §5.4 + §5.5

Still on the success screen: tap **Send review link (WhatsApp)**.

- Open the link on your phone: *her name, her treatment, your brand.*
  Rate 5 stars, tap "visible results", send.
- **The reward code appears instantly on her screen** — 10% off her next
  visit. *"That's the incentive loop: review in, reward out, redeemed at
  this same POS."*
- Switch to **Operations** (shahrukh@capture.cc) → **Reviews**: her review
  is already on the wall. Show the 4.6 average, the per-location and
  per-treatment scores, and the two **Follow up** flags — *"a 2-star at a
  partner clinic pages your team before it becomes a Google review."*

## Close (15s)

**Analytics**: "45 calls in two weeks, 40% booked, and what they ask about
most — glow packages and body contour. You're not guessing your demand
anymore."

*"From VYBERO's first hello to the Capture Circle reward — one system,
your brand, all four locations. This demo runs offline; production runs
on your own database. When do we start onboarding the partner clinics?"*

---

## If asked

- **"Is the before/after a guarantee?"** No — every render is watermarked
  illustrative; the doctor reviews before it leaves the building.
- **"What about our WhatsApp number?"** VYBERO connects to the Business
  API; the web widget is the same engine, so the demo behavior is the
  production behavior.
- **"Card payments?"** Recorded, not processed — your terminal keeps doing
  the money; the OS keeps the paper trail.
- **"Partner clinic onboarding?"** Discovery module (admin) generates the
  branded intake link — the same flow used to collect the current partners'
  wishlists.
